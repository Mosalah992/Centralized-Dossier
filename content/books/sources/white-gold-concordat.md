# The Concordat

Empire of the Medes — Aldmeri Dominion

Signed on the eleventh day of Sun’s Dusk, in the one hundred and seventy-fifth year of the Fourth Era, in the shadow of White-Gold Tower, in the Imperial City of Cyrodiil. The treaty marks the cessation of all hostilities between the Mede Empire and the Aldmeri Dominion, and takes effect immediately upon signing. It is binding on all subjects and allies of both powers.

# Preamble

We, Titus Mede II, Sovereign of the Mede Empire, Lord of Cyrodiil, Hammerfell, Skyrim, and High Rock, of the one part, and the First Council of the Thalmor of the Aldmeri Dominion, represented by Oriniel Ocato, vested with full diplomatic powers, of the other part:

Recognizing that the long and bloody war between our powers has inflicted incalculable damage on the peoples of Tamriel;

Driven by the desire for peace and order in the lands devastated by war;

Recognizing the legitimate authority and sovereignty of each of the Parties within the limits established by this Treaty, have solemnly agreed as follows:

# Chapter I. Termination of the War

## End of hostilities

From the moment of signing this Treaty, peace is established between the Empire of Mede and the Aldmeri Dominion. All military actions in all territories shall cease immediately.

1) Each party shall order the immediate cessation of hostilities and the withdrawal of all forces, within the borders established by this treaty.

2) Thalmor forces will begin a planned withdrawal from the Imperial City and all adjacent territories of Cyrodiil no later than three days from the date of signing.

3) Both the Aldmeri Dominion and Third Cyrodiilic Empire shall remain in non-aggression for the entirety of the lifespan of the White-Gold Concordat.

# Chapter II. Religious Obligations of the Empire

## Article 1. Prohibition of the Cult of Talos

In pursuance of this Treaty, the following restrictions are introduced throughout the entire territory of the Empire of the Medes, which are mandatory without any exceptions:

1) Talos, also known as Tiber Septim I, is no longer accorded a place among the Aedric deities of the Imperial pantheon. The Empire's public pantheon now numbers eight deities.

2) Throughout the Empire, the following is prohibited: public and/or private veneration of Talos as a divine being; holding religious services, rituals, and gatherings in his honor; storing and/or displaying symbols associated with his cult; and/or preaching his veneration.

3) The ban applies to all persons residing on the territory of the Empire, regardless of nationality, and race.

4) This prohibition does not extend to the recognition of Tiber Septim as a distinguished military leader, ruler, and founder of the Third Empire. Veneration of his memory as a historical figure, the study of his legacy, and invocation of his image as a symbol of imperial unity do not constitute a violation of this Treaty, provided they do not take the forms of religious worship expressly prohibited by this treaty.

## Article 2. Dissolution and Prohibition of the Order of Blades

As a condition for concluding peace, the Emperor shall order the immediate disbandment of the Order of Blades, an Imperial organization historically linked to the bloodline of Tiber Septim. Membership in the Order of Blades after the entry into force of this Treaty, as well as any forms of assistance to its members, or any attempt to re-establish the Order of Blades, is prohibited.

1) All active members of the Order are required to lay down their arms, hand over imperial powers, and cease operations within thirty days of the signing of the Treaty.

2) The Order's assets, documentation, and property are confiscated by the Empire and handed over to authorized representatives of the Thalmor Embassy for inspection.

3) The prosecution of former, current, and any future members of the order and persons associated with them shall be carried out by the Thalmor Embassy in accordance with the provisions of this Treaty, with the full cooperation of Imperial officials. Recognized higher authorities within the Empire are permitted to observe the trials of the Blades to ensure fair judgement.

4) Active membership, recruitment of new members, or re-establishment of the order's activities is punishable by death.

5) Financing, harboring or aiding members of the order is punishable by either death or the confiscation of property, depending upon the severity of the offense.

6) Failure to report a known member of the order is punishable by detention, and a fine of 1000 septims.

Persons who voluntarily disclose their previous affiliation with the Order and who cooperate with the Embassy have the right to petition for mitigation of liability at the discretion of the Embassy.

## Article 3. Powers of the Thalmor Embassy

In order to properly fulfill the obligations under Articles 1 and 2, the Aldmeri Dominion is granted the following rights within the territory of the Empire:

1) The Thalmor Embassy and its authorized agents and justiciars have the right to independently detect, investigate and apprehend persons violating the provisions of Articles 1 or 2, or else suspected of committing one or more of the offenses outlined in Articles 10 and 11.

2) Embassy agents are entitled to move freely throughout the Empire while fulfilling their duties. No imperial or local authorities have the right to hinder them in this regard.

3) The detained persons shall be transferred to the Embassy for trial and sentencing in accordance with Chapter VI of this Treaty.

4) Confiscated religious symbols and property related to the cult of Talos are transferred to the Embassy.

5) Any and all Blades members are subject to imprisonment (either by the Thalmor or by Imperial authorities), banishment, or death. Such sentences are determined by the severity of the Blades actions as discovered by both Justiciars and Inspectors of the Thalmor.

6) The Thalmor Embassy is authorized to house, arm, and maintain its own security force for the purpose of enforcing the articles set forth by this treaty, and protecting the agents of the Embassy from harm. This force is afforded the same diplomatic protections and privileges as all other Embassy personnel when carrying out official directives. Any deployments of this force within Imperial territory shall consist of no more than 6 individuals per escort, arrest, patrol, or any other official assignment. Any attempt to circumvent this limitation via the assignment of multiple deployments to the same directive shall attract liability under Article 7.2

## Article 4. Obligation to cooperate with the imperial authorities

The Imperial authorities - the Imperial Legion, local rulers, and the guards of the holds and provinces - are obliged to:

1) Provide the Thalmor Embassy and its agents with full cooperation upon official request of the Embassy. This extends to: providing armed escort, ensuring access to detainees, and extraditing persons suspected of violating Articles 1 or 2, or else suspected of committing one or more of the offenses outlined in Articles 10 and 11. Anything not listed explicitly herein may be requested but would not violate the terms of this treaty to deny.

2) Immediately transfer to the Embassy all persons detained by their own forces in cases of violation of this Treaty.

3) Not harbor persons declared wanted by the Embassy. Concealing such persons is considered concealment and harboring, which attracts liability under Article 11.

# Chapter III. Territorial Concessions

## Article 5. Cession of Southern Hammerfell

As a condition of the peace, the Mede Empire cedes to the Third Aldmeri Dominion the right of sovereign rule over the southern lands of the province of Hammerfell - from the Gilan Peninsula to the eastern walls of Elinhir - within the borders indicated on the map attached to this Treaty.

1) The transfer will be completed within one hundred and eighty days from the date of signing. Imperial military units and officials will withdraw from the transferred territories within the specified timeframe.

2) All laws and regulations of the Aldmeri Dominion shall immediately take effect in the transferred territories from the day of the actual transfer.

3) The civilian population of the transferred territories retains the right to life and property. Persons who do not recognize the Dominion's authority have the right to leave the territory within one year without hindrance.

# Chapter IV. Diplomatic Status of the Embassy

## Article 6. Establishment of the Thalmor Embassy

A Thalmor Embassy is established in each imperial province, as the exclusive property of the Aldmeri Dominion. The main Embassy is located in the Imperial City, Cyrodiil.

1) The buildings and surrounding areas of the Embassy enjoy extraterritorial status: they are subject to the laws of the Dominion, not those of the Empire or its subjects.

2) Any attempt by Imperial or local authorities to impose their legislation within the Thalmor Embassy is a gross violation of this Treaty.

3) No person who has taken refuge on the premises of the Embassy may be removed from there by imperial authorities. Refuge shall not be granted to individuals who have been issued an Imperial warrant of arrest, or lawful declaration of guilt, prior to the request for refuge. Violation of this clause shall attract liability under Article 7.2.

4) Any properties (in the form of land and/or permanent structures) within Imperial territory that are lawfully acquired by Embassy personnel are not afforded diplomatic status as an extension of the Embassy, and are subject to both provincial and Imperial legislation. The owner(s) of any such properties shall not be granted diplomatic immunity for crimes committed on said properties.

## Article 7. Diplomatic immunity

1) Accredited Embassy personnel, agents, and Thalmor justiciars are not subject to detention, arrest, or prosecution under the laws of the Empire and its subjects for actions undertaken while conducting official directives. This does not apply in the following cases that violate common law both Imperial and Provincial: Murder, Thievery, Invoking or participating in sedition, Unlawful obstruction of Imperial agents, Kidnapping, Daedra worship, Being a Daedric Abomination, Sacrificial Rituals of Man/Mer/Beastfolk, and Necromancy.

2) In the event any member of the Thalmor is accused of violating Imperial Autonomy in the form of a violation or overreach of Articles 3, 4, 6, 7, or 9, the Thalmor shall have primary jurisdiction to prosecute the accused upon written deposition by the Office of the High Magistrate to the Thalmor Embassy. The Empire shall be entitled to require such prosecution upon meeting the minimum standard of evidence established. In these cases, an Imperial Magistrate is required to be present unless such a right is foregone in writing by the Empire’s High Magistrate or Imperial Governor. If the accused is found guilty, or fails to appear before the relevant Thalmor authorities at the agreed-upon date, the individual(s) shall be extradited to the Empire’s Office of the High Magistrate for trial and/or sentencing.

3) Personal correspondence, documentation and property of Embassy employees are inviolable.

4) Inviolability extends to all actions by agents in the course of exercising the powers provided for in this Agreement.

5) Should diplomatic immunity be stripped through due process as described in Articles 7.1 and 7.2, the individual(s) losing their diplomatic immunity shall be granted an opportunity to safely return to the territory of the Aldmeri Dominion, provided death is not the lawfully ordained punishment.

Diplomatic immunity is suspended if the Dominion is recognized as an aggressor in a new armed conflict with the Empire. The procedure for such suspension is determined by the Empire's martial law legislation.

# Chapter V. Trade and Diplomatic Relations

## Article 8. Normalization of trade

1) Upon signing this treaty, trade restrictions introduced by each party during the war are withdrawn. The parties return to the trading conditions that existed before the Great War.

2) Subjects of the Dominion have the right to engage in trade activities within the territory of the Empire; subjects of the Empire have the right to engage in trade activities within the territory of the Dominion, in accordance with the laws of the receiving party.

## Article 9. Diplomatic relations

1) The parties exchange permanent diplomatic missions. Each has the right to accredit an ambassador to the court of the other, and to the courts of the other’s vassals. Such appointments’ powers, capabilities, and compensation within the vassals’ lands, unless otherwise granted within this treaty, are only guaranteed in form and scope by the court to which they are appointed.

2) The Parties undertake not to support, finance or shelter the enemies of the other Party operating on its territory.

3) Disagreements regarding the interpretation of this Agreement shall be resolved by direct negotiations between authorized representatives of the parties, represented by the council of Elders and the Thalmor Council.

# Chapter VI. Violations of the Concordat and Liability

This chapter establishes the elements of violation of the Concordat and the penalties applied by the Thalmor Embassy within its jurisdiction. Investigation and punishment are conducted by the Embassy independently in accordance with Dominion law. In the event the accused of any act herein is a member of Imperial nobility, an official, a priest of the Imperial Temple, a commanding officer of the Imperial Legion, or a member of a Jarl’s court, the Empire shall have primary jurisdiction to prosecute the accused upon written deposition by the Thalmor to the province’s Office of the High Magistrate, or equivalent supreme Imperial legal authority. The Thalmor shall be entitled to require such prosecution upon meeting the minimum standard of evidence established. In these cases, a Thalmor Justiciar is required to be present unless such a right is foregone in writing by the Embassy’s High Justiciar or First Emissary. If the accused is found guilty, or fails to appear before the relevant Imperial authorities at the agreed-upon date, the individual(s) shall be extradited to the Thalmor Embassy for trial and/or sentencing.

## Article 10. Violations of religious prohibitions

1) Heresy. Publicly professing Talos as a divine being of the Aedric pantheon, practicing his cult, organizing prayers, or holding religious gatherings is punishable by death.

1.1 The same act, committed by an official, priest, member of Imperial nobility, member of the Imperial Legion, or a person from a jarl's retinue, is punishable by death with confiscation of property.

2) Reestablishment of the cult. The creation or attempt to establish an organization, association, or secret society for the purpose of reviving the worship of Talos; the restoration of rituals or traditions associated with him - is punishable by death.

2.1 The same act committed by an official, priest, member of Imperial nobility, member of the Imperial Legion or a person from a jarl's retinue, is punishable by death with confiscation of property and posthumous deprivation of all titles and positions.

3) Cult artifacts. Storing, selling, transferring, or manufacturing items bearing the symbols of Talos or Tiber Septim - provided the act does not constitute an offense under paragraphs 1 or 2 of this article:

— storage: detention and a fine of 500 septims per item of contraband;

— sale or transfer: detention and a fine of 1000 septims per item of contraband;

— production: life imprisonment and a fine of 1500 septims per item of contraband.

3.1 The sale, storage, or production of cult artifacts, committed by an official, priest, member of Imperial nobility, member of the Imperial Legion, or a person from a jarl’s retinue, is punishable by deprivation of title, a ban on holding any titles in the future, and a fine of 2000 septims per item of contraband.

Any cult artifacts confiscated by local or imperial authorities are to be delivered to a Thalmor official or Justiciar at the earliest convenience. Local and imperial authorities shall not be prosecuted by Thalmor authorities for the possession of cult artifacts if they have intent to transfer any such item to the relevant persons, and can demonstrate that reasonable attempts to do so have been made.

## Article 11. Countering the Thalmor Embassy

1) Sedition. Inciting residents to oppose the Thalmor Embassy, its justiciars, or its security forces is punishable by imprisonment, and a fine of 500 septims or the confiscation of property of equivalent value.

1.1 The same act committed by an official, priest, member of Imperial nobility, member of the Imperial Legion, or a person from a jarl's retinue is punishable by deprivation of property, deprivation of title and a ban on holding any positions in the future.

1.2 The same act committed by multiple individuals in collaboration with one another is punishable by death.

2) Obstruction. Direct interference with the exercise of authority by Embassy agents in the form of a violation of Articles 3, 4, 6, or 7, is punishable by a fine of 1000 septims on the first offence, and death upon the second offense.

1.1 The same act committed by an official, priest, member of Imperial nobility, member of the Imperial Legion, or a person from the retinue of a jarl is punishable by a fine of 2,000 septims, deprivation of title, and a ban on holding any positions in the future.

3) Concealment and harboring. Concealment of persons wanted by the Embassy, failure to report known offenders, obstruction of an investigation, providing them with shelter or other assistance is punishable by imprisonment and a fine of 1000 septims.

3.1 The same act committed by an official, priest, member of Imperial nobility, member of the Imperial Legion, or a person from the retinue of a jarl is punishable by a fine of 5,000 septims, deprivation of title, and a ban on holding any positions in the future.

# Chapter VII. Procedure for the Agreement

## Article 12. Term and Termination

This Agreement is concluded on signing by both parties.

1) Either party has the right to notify the other of its intention to terminate the Agreement. Termination will take effect no earlier than one year from the date of notice.

2) A gross and systematic violation of the Agreement by one of the parties releases the other from the obligations assumed thereunder.

3) Failure by the Empire to comply with the agreed terms - primarily Articles 3, 4 and 8 - shall be considered grounds for termination of the Treaty by the Dominion.

## Article 13. Supremacy and Execution

1) This Agreement shall have priority over the domestic legislation of both parties in the part concerned.

2) Each party undertakes to bring its legislation into compliance with this Agreement within a reasonable time.

3) Compliance with the Treaty is mandatory for all subjects, officials and armed forces of each party.
