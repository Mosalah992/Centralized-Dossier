# Archive interaction requirements

## Navigation

- The archive shall expose only destinations already represented by the router and volume registry.
- Desktop navigation shall switch between a stable icon rail and a labelled panel, persisting the preference under `thalmor.sidebar.expanded`.
- Mobile navigation shall be an off-canvas drawer that closes on Escape, backdrop activation, or successful navigation and restores focus to its trigger.
- The active destination shall be conveyed by `aria-current` and by a non-colour-only marker.

## Dossier cards

- Archive books shall preserve their painted-cover presentation and real-link behavior while revealing truthful registry metadata and an opening action on hover, focus, or touch-capable layouts.
- Reports shall remain compact until their native disclosure control is activated.
- No metadata absent from the source models shall be invented.

## Reports seal

- The circular Archive Seal shall appear on the `/reports` gate and nowhere in the cabinet.
- Its front shall open to the existing Reports passphrase form through an accessible button and a CSS 3D flip.
- CSS shall own geometry and faces; centralized GSAP motion may own entrance and irregular glyph illumination.
- The seal shall remain fully usable with reduced motion and without JavaScript animation completion.

## Reports navigation motion

- Category and severity filters shall share a restrained liquid-ink active marker in the archive's gold, parchment, and dark-ink palette.
- The marker shall follow wrapped controls without a continuous JavaScript animation loop and shall become static under reduced motion.
