## Entry path

`web/src/main.tsx` starts the SPA. `web/src/App.tsx` chooses the Embassy room, the Gallery, a lazy volume view, or the dev-only editor. `web/src/router.ts` maps `/` (the room) and the room's own surfaces `/bookshelf`, `/bookshelf/:slug`, `/board`, `/board/:section`, `/scribe-table` (and the old `/lectern`, shown as `/scribe-table`), `/reports` (the Cabinet) and `/reports/:id` (one letter in it), plus `/solar` (High Command's room, a page of its own), `/gallery`, `/archives/:slug`, and development-only `/editor`. The retired mini-game and its route are intentionally absent from production and source.

There is no navigation rail and there are no painted covers (both retired 2026-10-20). The home page is the Embassy room (`web/src/room/`, a lazy chunk whose import App.tsx starts at module scope), the way into every page. Since 2026-11-02 it is pixel art drawn in code: a hall in one-point perspective (ceiling beams, panelled back and side walls, a gold-jointed stone floor and a navy runner, pillars at the back corners with ferns in gold urns, Dominion banners, blue-flame sconces), all one master picture (`scene.png`) with the furniture as sprites over it: bookshelf (Realm History, Chronicles, Arrests, Penal Code, White-Gold Concordat), bulletin board, the in-world clock and the two moons on a gold plate, the Orrery's painted glass globe with the Wheel turning inside it (`components/Orrery.tsx`, lazily, with anime.js) in a pixel cradle on the Cabinet (an instrument, not a link), two elven archways onto Aetherius (Roster & Chain of Command; Gallery & Hall of Honors), gold boughs over the top corners, Ancarion with his plate, the Cabinet, the Scribe Table with a candlestick at either hand, and the pinned note above the bookshelf. The sprites are drawn by `scripts/build-pixels.mjs` (`scripts/pixel/scene.mjs`, `objects.mjs`, `glyphs.mjs`, `raster.mjs`) into `web/src/assets/pixel/` with a manifest; places are art-pixel coordinates in `web/src/pixel/layout.ts`, shared by the build and the site. `web/src/pixel/scale.ts` picks the largest whole number of device pixels per art pixel at which the 420 by 180 core fits the window (`--px`), centres the core, and lets a wider or taller window see more of the walls, ceiling and floor; where that scale would be under 2.5 CSS pixels, or the window is portrait, the room stacks the same sprites in a column (data-layout `stack`). Animated sprites are strips of frames (candles, sconces, the arches' portals, the Cabinet's Fresh! drawer). Ancarion stays painted: his performer shows through the opening of a pixel frame (`portrait.png`, over the pixel plate), and the painting and frame swing open on their hinge as the Solar's door onto the painted glimpse of the Solar. Text stays live HTML in Pixelify Sans and, for the title, Jacquard 24. The archive's cursor is a pixel gauntlet pointing one finger up (`scripts/pixel/cursor.mjs`, 20 art pixels drawn at 2, 3, 4 and 6 screen pixels for 1x to 3x screens through `image-set()` in base.css). The 35 pixel icons (`web/src/pixel/icons/`, one sheet, `PixelIcon` at whole-number scales; built by `scripts/build-pixel-icons.mjs`) are in the tree but not yet placed on any page; their first uses are the board's sections, the Scribe wizard's categories, the Solar's tray and Notes, and the Cabinet's drawers. Ancarion keeps the Solar: pressing his portrait goes through for a reader holding the Solar's writ, and his plate takes the word from one who is not (`room/SolarPlate.tsx`); the Solar's door was taken down on 2026-10-23. Objects that lead to existing pages navigate there; the room's own surfaces open over it as Bruma-style takeovers with their own URLs. The old hero is `/gallery`, cut down to the Embassy's name, "Gallery", and the pictures (portrait, film, gallery), with the Dominion's name in its bar leading home. The visitor counter (the Register of Consultation) was retired on 2026-10-20. The room is built on the Bruma Joint Archives' room; until the Librarian backend lands, `/api/room/*` answers a JSON 404 and the board and the Scribe Table show their empty states. The Cabinet (`web/src/room/Cabinet.tsx` on the wall, `takeovers/Cabinet.tsx` opened) is a card catalogue of four drawers by age, its letters sealed in their wings' colours (`shared/wings.ts`), each unfolding in a GSAP film (`takeovers/cabinet/Letter.tsx`); a reader without the Reports writ is turned back to the room, where the Cabinet's slip opens for the word (Ancarion no longer answers for it). Every other page's first control is "Return to the Embassy". Changes of place run as view transitions (`web/src/transitions.ts`; the router preloads the next place first through `setRoutePreparer`), and Ancarion's portrait swings open as the Solar's door. Every volume on the bookshelf is a bound book read through `web/src/books/BoundBook.tsx` (spread, turning leaf, scrying search, guided reading), its leaves packed by `books/bind.tsx`; none is gated (`PortraitGate` and the Nirn sky were retired 2026-10-23). Ancarion's performance lives in `web/src/components/useAncarion.tsx` (his pure rules in `ancarion-script.ts`); his plate submits only to the Solar's server-verified gate and is not a second authentication mechanism. The Solar (`web/src/views/Solar.tsx`, route-only `solar.css`) is laid out after the First Emissary's sketch: notes, the war table with the realm map (the First Emissary's combined map of Skyrim and County Bruma, `Assets/Compined map.png`, prepared by `scripts/prepare-solar-map.mjs`), which spreads into a hand-built pan-and-zoom viewer where Dominion pawns and points of interest are set down, dragged and annotated, shared by everyone admitted; and the Clerk Table and Canonreeve's Desk, furnished from the Bruma Joint Archives' art but not yet wired. A reader reaching `/solar` without the writ is turned back to the room with the plate waiting.

Production is served from `https://thalmor-archives.com`. The root Pages middleware permanently redirects only `thalmor-archives.pages.dev`, preserving the path and query; branch-preview hostnames remain reachable so each feature can be tested before release.

The same root middleware applies the browser-security header set in `functions/lib/security.ts` to static, API, and redirect responses. `public/_headers` mirrors that policy for Pages static handling and gives immutable caching only to Vite-fingerprinted `/assets/*` files. Route-owned cache headers are never replaced.

## Runtime boundaries

| Boundary | Owns | Cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Browser (`web/`) | Reading experience and presentation | Access secrets or sealed text |
| Pages Functions (`functions/`) | Same-origin API, Sheets reads, gate, register read/write | Write the community sheet or run cron jobs |
| Shared (`shared/`) | Pure contracts and transformations | Depend on browser or Cloudflare-only APIs |
| Server (`server/`) | Google Sheets transport | Write Sheets |
| Chronicler (`chronicler/`) | Scheduled Discord collection into KV | Publish to Discord or compose the Chronicle |

## Archive content

The registry combines sheet-backed `VOLUMES` (now the Roster, the Chain of Command and the Hall of Honors; the Ledger, the Registry and the Calendar were retired 2026-10-20), repository-held `KEPT` volumes, and server-served `SEALED` volumes. Treat this classification as a security and deployment decision, not a display detail.
