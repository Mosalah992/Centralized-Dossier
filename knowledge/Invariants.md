# Invariants

1. **Read-only records:** The archive displays the sheet’s own values. It does not reconcile ledger or roster inconsistencies, and it never writes to Sheets.
2. **Gated doors protect themselves:** the Cabinet (Reports) and the Solar each check their own scoped signed cookie on every route, answer `private, no-store` and `Vary: Cookie` at every status, and fail closed without their secrets. Solar writes must also be same-site JSON. The Chronicle is public since 2026-10-23: its text stays server-side so it can be corrected without a rebuild, not to seal it.
3. **Public is not private:** `noindex` avoids search indexing; it is not authorization. Consider member-data exposure before adding fields or routes.
4. **Optional services fail quietly:** a missing collector token, KV binding, or D1 database must remove only its optional feature, not blank the archive.
5. **Shared storage identity:** `CHRONICLE_FILINGS` uses one KV namespace bound by both Pages and the Chronicler Worker.
6. **Asset provenance:** Publicly served art and fonts need confirmed web-embedding rights. Derived assets are generated by committed scripts.
7. **Motion and UI locality:** GSAP configuration remains centralized; anime.js drives only the Orrery's wheel (the room's orb, reached lazily) and never enters the entry or room chunks; the pixel room's sprites move only in drawn frames; three.js is not a dependency (retired 2026-11-02); bespoke CSS is authoritative outside `web/src/fluent/`.
8. **Browser policy is response-wide:** `functions/lib/security.ts` and `public/_headers` mirror the same restrictive policy. Root middleware adds it without replacing route-owned cache or `Vary` headers; gated APIs remain `private, no-store`.
9. **One grid, one palette:** the front page's art (all but Ancarion's painted performance and the Orrery's glass, which stay painted in pixel frames and cradles) is drawn in code (`scripts/build-pixels.mjs`) from `web/src/pixel/palette.ts` and placed by `web/src/pixel/layout.ts`; every sprite is opaque palette colours or nothing, and is shown only at a whole number of device pixels per art pixel (`web/src/pixel/scale.ts`). `test/pixel-art.test.ts` holds the line. Its faces, Pixelify Sans and Jacquard 24, are OFL.
